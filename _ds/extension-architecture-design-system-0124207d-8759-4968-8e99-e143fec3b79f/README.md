# Extension Architecture — Design System

A design system for **Extension Architecture**, one of London's leading architecture & planning consultancies, specialising in house extensions, loft conversions, planning applications, basement extensions and change-of-use projects. 16+ years experience, 1,800+ planning approvals.

- **Website:** extensionarchitecture.co.uk
- **Product surfaces:** (1) marketing website, (2) **internal staff portal** — `portal/` (Dashboard, Client Portal, Reports/Active Project Tracker, Settings)
- **Audience:** _Website_ — London homeowners considering an extension, loft or refurb. _Portal_ — EA's own delivery team (project managers, designers).
- **Tagline:** _London's Trusted Architects_

## Portal (internal product surface)

The `portal/` folder is a working internal ops tool, seeded from a prototype the **CEO** built and shared. The brief was to keep his exact build (markup, features, data, layout) and **layer the brand on top** — not to redesign. What was applied:

- **Type:** Playfair Display → **Fraunces** (display: titles + numerals), DM Sans → **Montserrat** (UI/body). **DM Mono** retained for tabular figures/timers (no brand mono exists). Self-hosted via `@font-face` in `portal/portal.css`.
- **Colour:** recoloured onto the brand tokens — Crimson `#8E1B18` primary, Noir ink ramp, three surface tiers, grove/amber/brick status colours, brand stage hues. (Tokens duplicated locally in `portal/portal.css` so the portal is self-contained; values mirror `colors_and_type.css`.)
- **Shape:** **every button** is the brand pill (`--radius-pill 100px`) — including the Designer Time Tracker controls; cards/inputs use `6–10px`.
- **Icons:** Lucide on the four new pages; the CEO's Reports page keeps its original emoji status glyphs (left per "don't change his build").
- **Alert banner kit:** a shared 10px-radius, 4px-left-border, bold-text banner shape used across all inline alerts. `--warning-*` tokens (below) for expected/non-error notices; `--info-*` tokens — `--info-bg #FFEDED`, `--info-wash #EBC6A3`, `--info-accent`/`--info-ink #8E1B18` (Crimson) — for informational status (e.g. "check your email" link-sent state). Danger/validation errors stay plain Crimson text with no banner shell.
- **Warning token:** `--warning-bg #FEF6E7` (pale amber surface), `--warning-wash #F0D48A` (hairline border), `--warning-accent #B8860B` (4px left border/icon stroke), `--warning-ink #7A5A00` (bold banner text/dismiss). Used for expected/non-error states — e.g. expired or already-used invite links — kept visually distinct from Crimson danger/error states.
- **Behaviour:** background scroll locks (`body.modal-open { overflow:hidden }`) when any modal opens; logo links to the website; custom select caret with equalised 11px insets.

> **Brand note:** the portal is an _internal tool_, so it deliberately diverges from the strict website rules in two ways the brand team signed off on — (a) Fraunces is used as a display face (the website is Montserrat-only), and (b) emoji remain on the Reports page (the website bans emoji). Don't carry either back into marketing surfaces.

## CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS

### Voice
**Warm-professional.** The audience is a London homeowner, often nervous about planning bureaucracy. Copy should feel like a calm, expert friend — _reassuring, expert, trustworthy_ — never cold, never salesy. Aspirational but practical: we can get your extension approved, we've done it 1,800 times, here's how.

### Person & pronoun
- **"We"** for the practice ("We are a small group of designers and thinkers…").
- **"You / your"** to address the homeowner directly ("Your property is feasible for Outbuildings…").
- Never "I". Never "the customer".

### Casing
- **Title Case** for page titles and nav items: _Loft Conversions, Get A Quote, Listed Building Services, Single Storey Extensions._
- **Sentence case** for body and long-form copy.
- **ALL CAPS, widely tracked (0.16em)** for eyebrow labels above headings — e.g. _RIBA ARCHITECTS IN LONDON_. Use sparingly; only as a small wayfinder label, not body.

### Punctuation & tone moves
- Ampersands encouraged: _Design & Build_, _Architecture & Planning_, _Do's & Don'ts_.
- En-dashes to scope: _Turn–Key_, _Single–Team Model_.
- "+" used in stats: _16+ years_, _1,800+ approvals_.
- British English spellings: _specialise, organisation, colour_.

### Copy examples (from Figma + PDF — use as voice reference)
- "Extension Architecture is one of the leading Architecture & Planning Consultant practices in London."
- "We are a small group of designers and thinkers who specialise in house extensions London, planning applications, building regulation drawings, loft conversions London and project management."
- "If you are looking for a reliable architecture firm in London to take you from creative direction to the ground up, Extension Architecture is here to help!"
- "Your property is feasible for Outbuildings & Garden structures under Permitted Development!"
- "A Decade of Architectural Design with Precision, Passion, & Purpose."
- Core values (PDF): _Turn-Key_, _One Stop Solution_, _Single-Team Model_.

### Key brand messages to reuse
- London's Trusted Architects
- End-to-end Design & Build
- On time, on budget
- Seamless process from feasibility to delivery
- 16+ years experience · 1,800+ planning approvals
- RIBA Chartered

### Do / Don't
- **Do** lead with reassurance, then credentials, then CTA.
- **Do** guide every page toward "Get A Quote".
- **Don't** use cold corporate jargon ("solutions provider", "stakeholders").
- **Don't** use emoji. Not on brand.
- **Don't** hype with superlatives — quiet confidence beats bluster.

## VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

### Palette
Primary: **Noir `#191919`**, **Frosty White `#FFFFFF`**, **Crimson `#8E1B18`**, **Bisque `#EBC6A3`**.
Secondary (vegetation / illustration only): **Grove `#1E4D25`**, **Sprout `#688F3D`**, **Sea `#188B8E`**, **Surge `#FFC554`**.

The site lives in **black / white / crimson**. Bisque is a warm break. Greens/teal/yellow are only for diagrams, illustrated maps and charts — never chrome.

### Type
**Montserrat, one family, across the whole marketing brand.** Medium 500 is the default weight (it is by FAR the most used — ~60% of all type in Figma is 17px/500). SemiBold 600 handles all headings. Body sits at 17px with 1.7 line-height. Tracking +0.010em on body; -0.010em on large headings; +0.16em all-caps for eyebrow labels.

**Portal surface — type pairing.** The new client portal pairs **Fraunces** (serif display, `--font-serif`) for headings/numerals with **Montserrat** (`--font-sans`) for body, forms, tables and dense UI. Fraunces is variable (opsz / wght / SOFT / WONK) — use `font-optical-sizing: auto` so large display sizes get the high-contrast cut and small text stays sturdy. Do **not** set Fraunces on dense UI text (labels, table cells, inputs) — readability drops; keep those Montserrat. The marketing site headings are unchanged (still Montserrat via `--font-display`).

### Backgrounds
- **Three surface tiers**: white (`#FFFFFF`) canvas, paper (`#FBFBFB`) raised card, bone (`#F3F3F3`) muted section.
- **Dark sections**: Noir `#191919` full-bleed — used for premium/credential sections.
- **Brand sections**: Crimson `#8E1B18` full-bleed — used for CTA blocks and conversion surfaces.
- **Imagery**: full-bleed hero photography of real London architecture (brick, sash windows, gardens, mid-day daylight). Over imagery, a **50% black overlay** (`rgba(25,25,25,0.5)`) keeps white text legible — this is the canonical pattern from the Figma buttons-on-image frame.
- **No gradients as decoration.** The only gradient is the 50%-black protection layer over photos. No bluish-purple fades. No radial spotlights.
- **No patterns, no textures, no noise.** Clean surfaces only.

### Imagery vibe
Per PDF §15: _"unposed, honest, and human… real people in real spaces"_. Natural daylight, warm-neutral, slightly muted. Not over-retouched. Not stock. Brick, timber, plaster, greenery. Occasionally AI-generated where real photos aren't available (PDF §17 — OK).

### Corner radii
- **5px** (`--radius-sm`) is the workhorse — cards, logo tiles, image tiles.
- **100px** (`--radius-pill`) — _every_ button and badge. Fully rounded capsules.
- **8–16px** for larger cards when needed.
- Never sharp 0px on interactive elements — it reads too brutalist.

### Buttons
Canonical pill, `169×49px`, `padding: 10px 30px`, `border-radius: 100px`, `font: Montserrat Medium 17px / 1.7 / +0.016em tracking`, label "Get A Quote" (or service-specific).

Variants (all from Figma `Button Elements UI`):
1. **Fill-Dark** — Noir bg, white text (on white surface).
2. **Fill-Red** — Crimson bg, white text (primary CTA on light surface).
3. **Outline-Dark** — white bg, 1px Noir border, Noir text.
4. **Outline-Red** — white bg, 1px Crimson border, Crimson text.
5. **Outline-Inverse** — Crimson bg, 1.5px white border, white text (on Crimson surface).
6. **Outline-on-Noir** — Noir bg, 1.5px white border, white text (on dark surface).
7. **Glass** — 1.5px white border, translucent fill, over imagery.
8. **Text link CTA** — label + 1.5px full-width underline. 5px underline = inactive, full-width underline = hover.

### Hover & press states
- **Buttons**: swap fill and stroke. Fill-Red on hover → darkens to `--ea-crimson-600` (#7A1714). Outline variants fill in to solid. Transition `var(--dur-fast)` (150ms) `ease-out`.
- **Icon circles** (arrow buttons): outline→fill. Stroke-icon → inverse-color filled-icon. See the `Icon` frame — default is 2px Crimson ring with the Lucide `move-up-right` arrow; hover is solid Crimson fill with the white arrow.hite arrow.
- **Text links**: underline expands from 5px to full width. Color stays Crimson.
- **Press**: subtle scale(0.98). No color flash. Keep it quiet.

### Borders
- **1px hairlines** `--ea-mist #D1D1D1` for dividers and subtle cards.
- **1px Noir** for dark outline buttons.
- **1.5px white** over imagery/dark surfaces (so it doesn't dissolve).
- **Dashed purple borders in Figma are tooling only** — they mark component bounding boxes. Never reproduce them in UI.

### Shadows
Minimal. Architecture brand — we don't float everything. Three elevations:
- `--shadow-1` (hairline): cards that sit on paper.
- `--shadow-2`: raised cards that lift over photography.
- `--shadow-3`: modals, lightbox overlays only.

### Animation
- Page loads & cards in: fade up 12px, 400ms `ease-out`, 60ms stagger.
- Hovers: 150ms color/fill.
- Carousels slide; no bouncing, no spring physics.
- No Lottie, no scroll hijacking, no parallax (the brand is warm-professional; not playful).

### Transparency & blur
- 50% black overlay on hero photography (protection layer).
- **No backdrop-blur.** Glass buttons are matte — just a white 1.5px border over a 20% white fill.

### Layout rules
- Fixed top nav, 80–100px tall, white or transparent-over-dark.
- Max content width **1320px**.
- Outer padding: `clamp(20px, 4vw, 64px)`.
- Generous vertical rhythm — sections `96–128px` tall on desktop, `64px` on mobile.
- Grids: 12-col desktop, 4-col mobile. Gutters `24–32px`.
- Stats appear as **4-up columns** with giant D6/D5 numerals and a small label.

### Card patterns
- **Service card**: 16:10 photo, 24px padding, h6 title, 15px body, text-link CTA. Hairline border on paper bg; no shadow at rest; `--shadow-2` on hover.
- **Testimonial card**: white, 24px padding, crimson opening quote-mark glyph at 42px, body at 17px, 1px hairline border.
- **Stat card**: numeral in D5 SemiBold + 15px Medium label underneath in Crimson.
- **Blog card**: image top, eyebrow label (date + category, all-caps crimson), h5 title, text-link CTA.

## ICONOGRAPHY

The brand does **not** ship a full icon set. Two icon patterns are used:

### 1. Circular arrow button (canonical)
From `/Page-1/Icon` in Figma — 56–64px circle, 2px Crimson border, Lucide **`move-up-right`** glyph centered. Hover state fills to solid Crimson with a white arrow. This is the primary "next", "read more" and "carousel advance" affordance.

All four directional arrows (up-right, left, right, down) use stock **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev/)** paths at 2px stroke — no hand-drawn arrows. `ui_kits/website/IconCircle.jsx` ships these directions.

Files:
- `assets/icon-arrow.svg` — arrow path (currentColor-driven, extracted from Figma).
- `assets/icon-arrow-white.svg` — white-fill version used on the dark/hover circle.

### 2. Hamburger / UI bits
The mobile nav hamburger is three 1.5px white/black rules, 23px wide, 6px gap — literally three `<span>` bars. Don't replace it with an icon-font burger.

### Everything else
All inline icons are **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev/)** at 2px stroke — checkmarks, chevrons, phone/mail, social, plus, download, play. No other icon family is sanctioned.-width 1.5, Crimson or Noir. _This is a substitution — flag for review._

```html
<script type="module">
  import { createIcons, icons } from "https://unpkg.com/lucide@latest/dist/esm/lucide.js";
  createIcons({ icons, attrs: { 'stroke-width': 1.5 } });
</script>
<i data-lucide="arrow-up-right"></i>
```

### No emoji, no unicode glyphs as icons
The PDF's do's-and-don'ts section explicitly steers away from decoration. Use the crimson arrow circle for emphasis; use Lucide at 2px stroke for inline.

### Logo lockups
- `assets/logo-black.png` — primary, on white/light surfaces.
- `assets/logo-white.png` — on Noir or Crimson surfaces.
- `assets/logo-red.png` — special uses (favicon, monogram).

The "e+a" mark can be isolated from the full lockup — clip to the leftmost ~22% for a monogram favicon. (Favicon source file was not provided — flag if you need a separate asset.)
